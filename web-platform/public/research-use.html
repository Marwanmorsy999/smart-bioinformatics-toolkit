Scope and limits

Research-use policy

This policy covers browser FASTA validation and statistics, BLAST TSV filtering, taxonomic consensus, the AI workspace plan and approval flow, the phylogenetics page, the research workspace metadata view and the synthetic benchmark walkthrough. No clinical, forensic or regulatory decision is supported.

Full statement

This platform is designed for research and educational use. Its outputs are computational evidence and do not constitute clinical diagnosis. Taxonomic assignments and other scientific interpretations depend on input quality, reference databases, methods and validation. Results do not independently confirm organism identity or pathogenicity and are not validated for clinical, forensic or regulatory decision-making. Users remain responsible for independent scientific assessment and interpretation.

Taxonomy confidence and reference coverage

Evidence fractions

Consensus

Consensus support is an evidence fraction, not a probability of correct identification. A 98.65% identity threshold is a screening reference, not a universal species boundary. BLAST similarity is evidence, not proof of species identity.

Reference coverage

Input dependent

Results depend on the BLAST TSV you supply and, optionally, a taxonomy mapping. Without a mapping, taxa are reported as unresolved; queries with no hits are reported as insufficient evidence; truncated candidate sets, multiple taxids and missing taxids are caveated. Local-agent taxa calls need a local BLAST database and are not claimed without one.

Difficult-group literature notes are references for review, not validated identification rules.

Phylogenetic hypotheses

Reported status

Not production-ready

MAFFT submission/completion was evidenced once; full multi-step execution is unverified; workflow not production-ready. Proposed steps are a plan, not evidence that a job ran.

Trees are hypotheses, not publication-grade inference without independent validation. Your FASTA is uploaded to Galaxy only after you approve the plan.

External execution and consent

Destination and approval

Per-step consent

Standalone tools parse data locally. A Worker plan request is sent only after you submit a request; heavy tools require a local agent, plan approval and per-step consent.

Nothing is sent to a remote service without a separate, dataset-scoped consent. Files leave the platform only with explicit, dataset-scoped consent. The data-transfer ledger and locality receipt record destinations, byte counts and consent references.

Report evidence quality

What a report records

Evidence, not conclusion

JSON, CSV and HTML exports carry parameters, thresholds, input hashes and limitations so an output can be judged on its evidence. A completed stage does not imply validated artifacts, and no output is labelled a conclusion.

Use each report as a record of what was run, not as an independent confirmation of identity or pathogenicity. Interpretation remains the user's responsibility.

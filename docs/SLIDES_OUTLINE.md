### Slide 1 — Title; research use only
- Title: **Smart Bioinformatics Toolkit — conservative 16S verdicts and a reproducible benchmark**.
- Subtitle: *Research and education use only. Not a clinical diagnostic device.*
- Thesis: turn raw BLAST similarity into an explained conservative verdict; measure the trade-off
  against baselines on real data. Numbers: none. External claims: none.
- Speaker notes: open with the research-only disclaimer; no numbers to cite.

### Slide 2 — Problem: top-hit names are the wrong species
- A plain top hit confidently names a species even when the true species is absent from the
  reference database; a fixed `98.65%` cutoff still names a wrong sibling that clears it
  (docs/PROJECT_SUMMARY.md). Number `98.65%` source: `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` (cutoff table label).
- Users need an explicit "insufficient evidence" outcome and an auditable comparison.
- External: unresolved species-level 16S [CEREUS, UNVERIFIED]; E. coli/Shigella [ECSHIG, UNVERIFIED].
- Speaker notes: `98.65%` from `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`; gap from `docs/PROJECT_SUMMARY.md`;
  literature claims UNVERIFIED, cited by ledger key.

- External: BLAST URL API [BLASTAPI, verified]; DB `16S_ribosomal_RNA` [DBNAME, verified];

- Speaker notes: no data numbers; architecture source `docs/PROJECT_SUMMARY.md`.

### Slide 4 — Real benchmark design
- Modes: held-out (true accessions removed) vs noisy (sanity). `n 200` per seed, seeds `1,2,3`,

  command + Limitations line). Brief calls for 3-5 seeds.
- Baselines: plain top hit; top hit + `98.65%` cutoff; Toolkit verdict — source
  `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` (result table labels). Real local BLAST+ vs curated bacterial 16S RefSeq;
  taxonomy from NCBI taxdump (docs/PROJECT_SUMMARY.md). External: [REFSEQ, verified], [BENCH, verified].
- Speaker notes: all numbers from `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`; outputs in `benchmark_data/` (untracked).

### Slide 5 — Held-out results (headline)
Values from `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` ("Real results - held-out (headline)"):

| Method | Calls/200 (mean) | Wrong calls (mean) | Wrong rate among calls | Abstentions (mean) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plain top hit | 200.0 | 143.3 | 0.717 | 0.0 |
| Top hit + 98.65% cutoff | 87.7 | 38.0 | 0.431 | 112.3 |
| Toolkit verdict | 49.7 | 12.7 | 0.257 | 150.3 |

- Wilson 95% CI, correct-call rate (seed 1): top [0.2315, 0.3564], cutoff [0.4374, 0.6321],
  verdict [0.6113, 0.8445] — source `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` (CI line).
- Takeaway: far fewer confident wrong calls, but species called much less often
  (docs/PROJECT_SUMMARY.md). External: [BENCH, verified].
- Speaker notes: all table/CI numbers from `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`; verdict is more conservative,
  not generally more accurate — compare call rates with wrong counts.

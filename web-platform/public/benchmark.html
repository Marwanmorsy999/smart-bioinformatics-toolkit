Risk-coverage analysis

A walkthrough of the risk-coverage sweep calculation using synthetic demonstration data from shared/test-cases/risk_coverage_cases.json. Real held-out numbers are in benchmark_data/RESULTS.md.

Synthetic demonstration — not a performance measurement

The sweep varies the species screening identity and shows species call rate against wrong rate. Lower wrong rate at the same call rate is better; abstentions are counted in the table.

How to read it

What is and is not measured

What the sweep shows

  • Five fixed synthetic cases are re-evaluated at thresholds of 97.0, 98.0, 98.65, 99.0 and 99.9% identity.
  • A species call requires a top species name, a non-null identity at or above the threshold, and query coverage of at least 80%.
  • Call rate is calls divided by all cases. Overall wrong rate is wrong calls divided by all cases, so abstentions remain in the denominator.
  • Wrong rate among calls divides wrong calls by calls only; it is not available when no calls are made.
  • The Calls and Wrong columns are counts out of five; lower wrong rate at the same call rate is better.

What it does not measure

  • These are fixture values, not performance on held-out or real samples.
  • Correct and wrong are judged only against the fixture’s supplied true_species labels; this is not proof of species identity.
  • No confidence intervals, uncertainty estimates or generalisation beyond these five cases are reported.
  • Query coverage is held at 80%; the sweep varies only the identity threshold, so other pipeline parameters are not compared.
  • No clinical validity or diagnostic claim follows from this illustration.

Limitations of this demonstration

The five threshold points are chosen to illustrate how the rates move, not to establish a rule: differences between adjacent points are properties of this small fixture. Treat every value on this page as an illustration of the calculation, never as an accuracy estimate. For measured results, consult benchmark_data/RESULTS.md.
